THE STORY

"My chat app is end-to-end encrypted. Why is my photo in danger?"

Chat encryption only protects the tunnel while files travel. The real leaks happen before you send and after they receive—across WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Slack, and Discord.

✕ THE STANDARD CHAT TRAP
  • 1. Your Camera Roll: The second you snap a photo of your NIN or bank card, your phone saves it. iCloud or Google Photos uploads it to train cloud AI and index your private data.
  • 2. App Compression & Cache Leaks: Chat apps recompress photos into lossy formats and store unencrypted local thumbnails in shared phone cache directories and cloud chat backups.
  • 3. Permanent Recipient Copy: Your private document sits in someone else's gallery forever. If they lose their phone or let someone browse their gallery, your data is exposed.
✓ HOW F.Y.E.O BREAKS THE CHAIN
  • 1. Zero-Storage Secret Camera: Photos are captured directly into volatile browser RAM. Your phone's gallery and cloud backups never see the photo.
  • 2. Visual Noise Scrambling: Your document is scrambled into static noise in your browser via Web Workers. Chat servers and eavesdroppers only see random pixels.
  • 3. Auto-Burn & Decrypt Shield: The recipient views the photo with a 30s auto-burn timer and tab-switch auto-blur. Once viewed, the memory is wiped to zero.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions

Can anyone decrypt my image without the password?

No. FYEO uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 rounds and NIST P-256 asymmetric ECDH. Without the exact passphrase or private device key, the image is mathematically indistinguishable from random visual noise.

Can I leave the scrambled image open in my browser or saved on my device?

Yes, it is 100% safe. Once scrambled, the image is pure high-entropy visual static. To anyone glancing at your screen, roommates, or cloud AI indexing tools (like Google Photos or Apple Intelligence), it is just meaningless random pixels. You can leave the tab open in your browser or keep the file in your downloads folder for months—nobody can glean a single pixel of your document until you enter the secret password to decrypt it.

Why must I send as a "Document" or "File"?

When you send a standard photo on WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, or Slack, the app recompresses it into a lossy JPEG to save bandwidth, permanently altering pixel values and breaking cryptographic integrity. Sending as a "Document" or "File" transmits the raw PNG bitstream with 100% pixel fidelity.

Is F.Y.E.O free and open source?

Yes, 100% free with no accounts, no cookies, no third-party tracking, and no paywalls. The cryptographic specification and source code are open on GitHub.
